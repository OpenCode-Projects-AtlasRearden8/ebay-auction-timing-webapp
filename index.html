Weekend windows

For flea-market and garage-sale flippers: you source on the weekend and list while it's fresh. This is the rhythm that compounds — pick Saturday, list Sunday evening, repeat.

The flipper rhythm

  • Saturday morning: garage sales, estate sales, flea markets. Buy clean, complete, photographed-ready items.
  • Saturday afternoon: photograph everything in one batch. Same background, natural light.
  • Sunday: research comps, write listings, schedule them to end next Sunday evening at the highest-ranked hour.
  • Sunday evening slot: by next Sunday your items end at the peak time, and the cycle repeats with money in your pocket.

Why it works

  • Sunday evening rules the roost. Decades of seller habit put peak browsing from about 5–9 PM in the buyer's time zone. Aim near the top: 6, 7 or 8 PM.
  • Avoid the dead zones. Overnight hours and Saturday daytime — buyers are asleep or at a garage sale. Ending Saturday at 1 PM means bidding against a flea market.
  • Weekday evenings are a solid Plan B. Mon–Thu 6–9 PM works well for commodity items where buyers search, wait, and pounce.
  • Relists are a rotation, not a repost. Change the ending evening when you relist. Same-slot relists inherit the same result.
  • Money math lives in the sibling tool. Pair this with ebay-profit-calculator-cli for fees and margin; this app owns the when, that one owns the how much.
  • Everything is local. No accounts, no scraping, no network calls. Your data sits in this browser's localStorage; clear it any time from the schedule page.

Experience differs from market to market. The chips are starting points, not laws — keep your own category notes and let them override the defaults.
